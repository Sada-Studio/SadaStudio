House of Hush

Sleepwear & Loungewear Brand

BrandingArt Direction

House of Hush is a sleepwear and loungewear brand with a bold, feminine attitude. Sada developed a fashion-led visual identity centered on an elegant calligraphic wordmark and monogram, paired with a rich palette of burgundy, blush pink, cream, and soft blue. Repeated script patterns, tactile fabric applications, garment tags, and intimate editorial photography extend the identity across every touchpoint, balancing the softness of comfort with a confident and distinctive visual presence.
