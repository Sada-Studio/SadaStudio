Karaj

Lebanese Mobile App

Branding

Karaj is a Lebanon-based platform that connects drivers with nearby garages — making it faster and easier to find trusted automotive help and streamlining the link between workshops and customers. The brand identity was developed end-to-end with a minimal, performance-driven approach inspired by McLaren and the automotive world. From the logo and visual language to key brand applications, Karaj was shaped to feel precise, premium, and engineered for motion.
