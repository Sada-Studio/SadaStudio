M FIT

Personal Training & Fitness Brand

BrandingSocial Media

For M FIT, a personal training brand, Sada developed the logo, visual identity, and social media direction. The logo pairs a fluid, organic “M,” inspired by the movement and curves of the human form, with a bold, structured “FIT” that represents discipline and strength. Its forward-leaning italic construction introduces a sense of speed, momentum, and progress. Together, these contrasting elements create a dynamic identity that balances freedom of movement with the commitment behind training.
