Marsa Inn

Seaside Seafood Restaurant & Guesthouse in Lebanon

BrandingArt Direction

Marsa Inn is a refined seaside destination in Lebanon, bringing together a seafood restaurant and intimate guest rooms by the beach. Sada developed a visual identity inspired by the textures, colors, and character of the Mediterranean, balancing deep sea blues and warm neutrals with a bold wordmark, playful marine iconography, and a bilingual typographic system. Extended across menus, hospitality touchpoints, and a grainy direct-flash photography direction, the identity positions Marsa Inn as an elevated coastal escape for dining, staying, and slowing down.
