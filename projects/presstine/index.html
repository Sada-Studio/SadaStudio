Presstine

Lebanese Custom & Ready-Made Stamp Brand

BrandingArt Direction

Presstine is an online shop offering custom and ready-made stamps through a distinctly Lebanese lens. Sada built the brand from the ground up, developing its logo, color palette, typography, graphic style, and photography direction. The logo draws on the fig “tine” in Arabic embedded within the brand’s name. Bright colors, youthful compositions, and intentionally imperfect details give the identity a tactile, handmade quality that reflects the unique irregularities and character of stamped impressions.
