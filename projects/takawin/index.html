Takawin

Lebanese Perfume Brand

BrandingPackaging

Takawin is a perfume brand that translates astrology into scent — crafting fragrances inspired by horoscopes and personal archetypes, where each blend feels like a signature aligned with the stars. The brand identity was built from the ground up, from logo creation to typographic direction, establishing a distinctive visual world that feels mystical yet modern. From the refined type system to the packaging design, every touchpoint was crafted to turn each perfume into a collectible ritual — expressive, intentional, and made to feel personal.
