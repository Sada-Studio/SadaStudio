The Super Power Father

Interactive Fatherhood & Parenting Workshop

BrandingEditorialPresentation

The Super Power Father is a workshop designed to help fathers approach parenting with greater confidence and connection. Sada developed a warm, energetic visual identity built around a bold color palette, expressive typography, playful graphics, and a relatable photography style. We then extended the identity across the full workshop experience, creating an interactive booklet with exercises designed to be cut out and used as activity cards, alongside a cohesive presentation deck to guide each session.
