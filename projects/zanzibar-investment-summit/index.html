Zanzibar Investment Summit

Investment Summit in Zanzibar

Event Branding

For the Zanzibar Investment Summit, Sada developed a complete visual identity rooted in the island’s cultural heritage. The logo draws inspiration from the intricately carved doors of Zanzibar’s historic old city, translating their ornamental forms into a contemporary symbol that also incorporates the letters “ZIS.” A culturally inspired color palette and supporting visual direction extend this concept across the summit’s communications, creating an identity that feels distinctive, relevant, and unmistakably connected to Zanzibar.
